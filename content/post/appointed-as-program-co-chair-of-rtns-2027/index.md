I am pleased to share that the **RTNS Steering Committee** has appointed me as a **Technical Program Co-Chair of the 2027 International Conference on Real-Time Networks and Systems (RTNS 2027)**, together with **Catherine Nemitz**.

RTNS is an established venue for research on real-time systems, networks, scheduling, and timing predictability, bringing together researchers working on both theoretical foundations and practical systems.

I am grateful to the Steering Committee for their trust and look forward to working with Catherine and the broader real-time systems community to put together a strong and engaging technical program for RTNS 2027.

More details about the conference and the call for papers will follow!

We are pleased to announce that two of our papers have been accepted for presentation at the **Design, Automation and Test in Europe Conference (DATE 2027)**.

Both papers address challenges in racetrack-memory computing, ranging from efficient and reliable neural-network execution to data-placement optimization.

* **Misalignment-aware Binarized Neural Networks on Racetrack Memories**\
  Leonard David Bereholschi, Simon Kurz, Mikail Yayla, Asif Ali Khan, Kuan-Hsun Chen, and Jian-Jia Chen.

  This paper builds on our previous work presented at SAMOS and introduces the idea of misalignment awareness in the training phase. 
* **SHIFTLESS: Spectral Seeding and Median-Guided Refinement for Racetrack-Memory Data Placement**\
  Elijah Seth Cishugi, Asif Ali Khan, Kuan-Hsun Chen, and Marco Ottavi.

  This paper investigates data-placement optimization for racetrack memories and results from our ongoing collaboration across the University of Twente, TU Dresden, and Paderborn University.

Congratulations to all co-authors!

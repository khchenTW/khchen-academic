I am honored to be recognized as an **Outstanding TPC Member at DAC 2026** for going above and beyond to support the conference.

Serving on a technical program committee is one of the less visible but essential parts of academic service. It involves carefully reviewing submissions, engaging in technical discussions, and helping the committee reach fair and well-grounded decisions. I am grateful to the DAC 2026 TPC leadership for this recognition.

DAC 2026, *The Chips to Systems Conference*, is being held in Long Beach, California, from July 26–29, 2026.

It has been a privilege to contribute to the technical program, and I look forward to continuing to support the DAC community.

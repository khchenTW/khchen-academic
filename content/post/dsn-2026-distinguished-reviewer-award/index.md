I am honored to receive the **DSN 2026 Distinguished Reviewer Award**.

DSN 2026 took place in Charlotte, North Carolina, USA, on June 22–25, 2026. Unfortunately, I could not attend in person this year due to teaching duties, so I missed the announcement during the conference opening and gala dinner — and only learned about the award afterwards.

Many thanks to the DSN 2026 Program Committee Co-Chairs, Lydia Y. Chen and Miguel Correia, and to the DSN community for this kind recognition.
